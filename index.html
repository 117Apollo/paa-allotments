About the association

A place to grow, together.

Plots at the Perdiswell Allotment Association are cultivated for fruit, vegetables and flowers. From spring through to autumn, the site becomes a hive of activity as plot-holders tend their plots, share advice and enjoy the outdoors.

Our community spans young families introducing children to the joy of growing from seed, through to experienced gardeners with decades of knowledge. We hold an Annual General Meeting each year and organise community days when the weather permits.
